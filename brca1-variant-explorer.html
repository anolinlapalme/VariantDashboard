Data: Ensembl VEP REST and Compara, gnomAD v4.1, NCBI ClinVar, BRCA Exchange (ENIGMA), UniProt, AlphaFold DB, MaveDB (Findlay 2018, Starita 2015/2018, Adamovich 2022; CC0), ClinGen CSpec registry (ENIGMA BRCA1/2 VCEP v1.2 Tables 4 and 9 and supplementary tables), PDBe and RCSB; Behl et al. 2020 (French-Canadian founders); Spurdle et al. 2014 (pathology likelihood ratios). With a data release file (brca1-data.js, built by build_brca1_db.py) these are read from that dated file; without it they are queried live. Rules: CanVIG-UK consensus specification v3.30 and BRCA1/BRCA2 gene-specific guidance v1.40; ENIGMA VCEP v1.2. Components: Nightingale (EBI, MIT), Mol* via the PDBe Mol* plugin (MIT/Apache-2.0), SheetJS (Apache-2.0); mutant models built offline with PDBFixer + OpenMM and analysed with Biopython. Every value on this page comes from a named source or a fixed rule; nothing is generated by a language model. The CanVIG-UK worksheet lists codes for review and does not classify variants.
